# The FAQ powered by Notion

**Open it:** <https://pikooli.github.io/slide/notion-support/>

An internal Hiway presentation (7 slides) about publishing help articles straight from Notion into the app, with no development step.

## Slides

1. **Cover**: the FAQ powered by Notion.
2. **Where we started**: publishing an article took a developer, two teams and days of back-and-forth.
3. **The solution**: support writes in Notion, changes a status, and the article is live in the app.
4. **Three immediate wins**: no dev cost, faster releases, unchanged support workflow.
5. **Boosted by AI**: articles written, formatted and published with Claude & Codex.
6. **The leverage effect**: a shared business knowledge base, readable by everyone, editable by owners.
7. **Before / Now**: the impact in short.
